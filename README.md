#Circles UBI Research Report

##Overview
A professional research report analyzing Circles (CRC), a decentralized trust-based Universal Basic Income protocol built on Gnosis Chain. This report covers the project's mechanics, team, token model, market positioning, and viability from an institutional research perspective.

##Contents

Executive Summary
Project Overview & TAM/SAM/SOM analysis
Team & Backers
Fundamentals (user data, trust network)
Token mechanics & pricing
Competitors & Partnerships
Milestones, Catalysts & Risks
Socials & Community analysis
Chart analysis
Weighted scoring methodology

##Key Findings

Overall weighted score: 5.38/10
Innovative trust-based UBI model with strong Gnosis ecosystem backing
Early-stage adoption with ~8,666 V2 users at time of writing (August 2025)
Regulatory uncertainty and unclear path to sustainability remain primary risks
